# Privacy, Data Governance & Accessibility Checklist

## Privacy and data handling
- [ ] Data inventory and purpose are understood.
- [ ] Only necessary personal data is collected.
- [ ] Sensitive fields are encrypted or otherwise protected where appropriate.
- [ ] Access to personal data is role-scoped and audited.
- [ ] Retention and deletion policies are defined.
- [ ] Exports, backups, logs, and analytics do not leak unnecessary personal data.
- [ ] Consent and privacy notices are appropriate to the product and jurisdiction.
- [ ] Third-party scripts/services and data transfers are understood.
- [ ] Data subject requests and account deletion are handled where applicable.
- [ ] Secrets and personal data are not placed in URLs or analytics events.

## Accessibility and usability
- [ ] Forms have labels, clear errors, and keyboard operation.
- [ ] Color is not the only way to communicate state.
- [ ] Contrast and focus indicators are usable.
- [ ] Images have appropriate alternative text.
- [ ] Dialogs and menus support keyboard and assistive technology.
- [ ] CAPTCHA has an accessible alternative.
- [ ] Responsive layout and zoom are usable.
- [ ] Language and document structure are declared correctly.

Do not make legal-compliance claims. Identify relevant questions and recommend
qualified review where necessary.
